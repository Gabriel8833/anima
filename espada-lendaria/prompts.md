# Prompts de geração (imagem/vídeo 3D)

Cole o **bloco de personagem** e o **bloco de estilo** em todo prompt para manter a continuidade.
Gere primeiro uma folha de personagem de Arthur e use-a como imagem de referência em todas as cenas.

**Personagem (Arthur):** young peasant man, ~18 years old, messy chestnut-brown hair, freckled face, cream linen tunic, brown leather belt, green scarf, worn brown boots. Same face and outfit in every shot.

**Estilo:** stylized 3D animated film look, cinematic lighting, vertical 9:16, 1080x1920, shallow depth of field, constant camera motion, no static shots.

**Paleta:** forest = dark blue-green with teal mist; gold ONLY on the sword and the scene 5 light burst (scene 1 sunset is the warm exception outside the forest).

---

## Cena 1 — O reino (0–6 s)
Fast aerial drone shot flying over the vast kingdom of Eldoria at golden sunset, castles on cliffs, banners whipping in the wind, armored warriors marching through the main gates, camera rushes toward the castle then tilts up to the tallest tower. Quick cuts, energetic motion.

## Cena 2 — A floresta (6–13 s)
Low-angle tracking shot behind armored warriors entering a cursed forest, twisted gnarled trees, thick blue-green mist, yellow eyes blinking in the shadows, a large creature shifting between the trunks, growing tension, ends dissolving into the mist.

## Cena 3 — Arthur (13–21 s)
[Personagem] Close-up of Arthur's face walking alone through the same dark blue-green forest, fear in his eyes but determined; then side tracking shot of his boots stepping on the dirt trail; a single shaft of light breaks through the canopy.

## Cena 4 — O guardião (21–29 s)
[Personagem] In a forest clearing, Arthur faces a giant knight in black armor with a horned helmet and one glowing red eye. Low angle on the giant, fast cuts, camera shake on each strike. Arthur dodges, rolls, uses the terrain; the knight collapses into black dust. Ends in a white flash.

## Cena 5 — A espada (29–39 s)
[Personagem] Slow push-in as Arthur approaches an ancient stone altar where the sword Excalion is embedded; close-up of his hand gripping the hilt, deep breath, he pulls it free; a burst of golden light floods the forest with particles and a shockwave; camera pulls back explosively; Arthur's face in awe. Ends in a golden flash.

## Cena 6 — O gancho (39–45 s)
[Personagem] Wide shot from behind Arthur holding the glowing sword; beyond the mountains a colossal shadow with two red eyes rises from the mist; the forest darkens; Arthur slowly turns his head; fast pull-back, cut to black.
